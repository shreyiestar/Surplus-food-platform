No Plate Left Empty, No Worker Left Unpaid

We connect restaurants, hostels, college canteens, households and weddings with verified NGOs — delivered by paid cooperative workers, not volunteers.

🍲 Donors

Restaurants, hostels, canteens, households, weddings post surplus food in seconds.

🤝 NGOs

Verified organizations get matched to nearby donations automatically.

🚴 Cooperative Workers

Earn a fair, transparent fare for every pickup-and-delivery job.

How It Works

1

Post

A donor lists surplus food with pickup location and expiry time.

2

Match

The platform auto-matches the nearest verified NGO and cooperative worker.

3

Deliver

The worker picks up and delivers the food, earning a fair fare.

4

Impact

Food is saved, an NGO is fed, and a worker is paid — all in one trip.

About SharePlate

Built for Smart India Hackathon — Problem Statement 26089. Instead of another food-donation app, SharePlate is a community service marketplace powered by cooperative workers — generating fair local employment while solving local food waste, one pickup at a time.
